## Are crypto deposits free on OKX?

In the normal case, yes. OKX states that users do not pay a fee when cryptocurrency is deposited into an OKX account. The fee is generally paid by the party sending the crypto, which may be another exchange or a private wallet.

That does not mean every crypto transfer is cost-free from start to finish. The sending platform may charge a withdrawal fee, and the blockchain may require a network fee. Those charges happen before the funds arrive at OKX.

For example:

- You withdraw BTC from another exchange.
- The other exchange deducts its withdrawal fee.
- The transaction is broadcast to the Bitcoin network.
- OKX receives the remaining amount without charging a separate deposit fee.

The amount that arrives at OKX can therefore be lower than the amount you entered on the sending platform. That difference is usually a sender-side withdrawal fee, not an OKX deposit fee.

### The network must match

When depositing crypto, the asset and network must be selected correctly. OKX requires the token and deposit network to match the network used by the sending wallet or exchange. If a token or network does not appear on the OKX deposit page, OKX says that deposit route is not supported.

This is more important than saving a small fee. Sending an asset through the wrong network can delay recovery or make the funds difficult to recover. Before confirming a transfer, compare:

- The exact token name
- The selected blockchain network
- The deposit address
- Any memo, tag, or destination field
- The minimum deposit amount

OKX also warns that deposits below the minimum limit may not be credited automatically. Recovery may be possible for some assets, but additional recovery fees can apply.

## What are OKX cash deposit fees?

Cash deposit fees are more variable than crypto deposit fees. OKX says the available currencies and payment methods depend on your country or region, and the options shown in your account may differ from those shown to another user.

When you choose a cash deposit method, OKX displays:

- The processing fee
- The minimum and maximum deposit limits
- The remaining account limit
- The expected processing time
- The amount that will actually be credited

The fee can use one of three structures:

1. **Flat fee**
   A fixed charge regardless of the deposit size.

2. **Percentage fee**
   A percentage of the amount deposited.

3. **Percentage plus flat fee**
   A percentage charge combined with a fixed amount.

For instance, if a payment method charges 2% and you deposit $500, the processing fee would be $10. If it charges 1.5% plus $1, the same deposit would cost $8.50. These examples explain how the fee model works; they are not a fixed OKX rate for every account or payment method.

The actual amount shown in the deposit preview is the figure to rely on. OKX says that the processing fee is deducted from the deposit amount. If you initiate a $100 deposit and the preview shows a $2 fee, approximately $98 is credited before any other bank-side charges.

## U.S. domestic wire deposit fees

OKX says it does not charge a fee for USD deposits sent through a U.S. domestic wire transfer. Your bank may still charge an outgoing wire fee, and intermediary banks can apply their own charges in some circumstances.

Domestic wire transfers are generally used for larger deposits because they can provide higher limits or faster settlement than some other methods. OKX states that domestic wire deposits commonly take between one and three business days, depending on the bank.

Before sending a wire:

- Open the USD deposit instructions inside OKX.
- Copy the recipient name and address exactly.
- Copy the routing number and account number carefully.
- Check the minimum and remaining deposit limits.
- Confirm that the sending bank account belongs to you.
- Review whether your bank charges an outgoing wire fee.

The deposit instructions are account-specific. Do not use bank details copied from an old screenshot, forum post, or another user’s transaction. OKX says deposits sent below the minimum or above the remaining limit may be returned, and the return can involve additional bank charges.

For a small deposit, a bank wire fee can be disproportionate. If your bank charges $20 or $30 for a wire, the supposedly free OKX deposit may still be expensive in practice. For larger transfers, the same bank fee may matter less as a percentage of the total.

Card deposits are convenient, but the fee is not always the lowest. OKX supports card-based cash deposits for some currencies and regions, including Visa and Mastercard routes where available. The availability and exact terms depend on the account and local service coverage.

A card deposit can be useful when:

Before confirming a card deposit, check:

## Deposit fees are separate from trading fees

A common source of confusion is treating every OKX charge as a deposit fee. There are several different cost categories:

- **Deposit fee:** The charge for moving funds into OKX.
- **Withdrawal fee:** The charge for moving funds out of OKX.
- **Trading fee:** The maker or taker fee charged when an order executes.
- **Network fee:** A blockchain transaction cost, often associated with withdrawals.
- **Payment-provider fee:** A charge from a bank, card issuer, or third-party payment processor.
- **Conversion spread or exchange rate difference:** The cost embedded in converting one currency or asset into another.

A free crypto deposit does not mean the later trade is free. Likewise, a free USD bank deposit does not remove spot, futures, conversion, or withdrawal charges.

OKX’s fee documentation states that deposits, withdrawals, and card transactions are priced separately from trading fees. It also states that banks and payment providers may set their own charges.

## What can cause a deposit to be delayed or rejected?

A deposit may not arrive immediately even when the displayed fee is zero. Common causes include:

- The blockchain has not reached the required confirmations.
- The wrong network was selected.
- A memo or tag was missing.
- The deposit was below the minimum.
- The bank account name does not match the OKX account.
- The transfer exceeded the remaining daily or monthly limit.
- The payment method is not available in the user’s jurisdiction.
- The account has not completed identity verification.
- The account is frozen, restricted, or under review.

For crypto, check the transaction hash on the relevant blockchain explorer before contacting support. For cash deposits, check the deposit history and bank transfer status. OKX’s help documentation notes that cash deposit limits can vary by method and verification level, with daily, weekly, or monthly limits resetting on a rolling basis rather than necessarily at midnight.
